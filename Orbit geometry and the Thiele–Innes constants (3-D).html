Orbit geometry and the Thiele–Innes constants

Orbit of the primary about the barycentre (primary-frame convention of spectroscopic orbits and of orblet), in the frame used in the notes: x = North, y = East (position angle θ from N through E), z = line of sight, positive away from the observer. The companion lies on the opposite side of the barycentre; its orbit relative to the primary has the constants −(A,B,F,G), i.e. ω+180° at the same Ω. Formulas

X = cos E − e,  Y = √(1−e²) sin E
x = A X + F Y     (North)
y = B X + G Y     (East)
z = C X + H Y     (away)
A = a( cosω cosΩ − sinω sinΩ cos i)
B = a( cosω sinΩ + sinω cosΩ cos i)
F = a(−sinω cosΩ − cosω sinΩ cos i)
G = a(−sinω sinΩ + cosω cosΩ cos i)
C = a sinω sin i,  H = a cosω sin i
w = x cosψ + y sinψ

Companion relative to primary: −(A,B,F,G)
